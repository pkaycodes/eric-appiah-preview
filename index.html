01 The through-line Accra · Since 2016

Technology is only impressive when it works for people.

For more than a decade, Eric Appiah has worked at the point where technology, business and sustainability meet — helping organisations adopt AI deliberately, secure what they build, and turn sustainability from a report into practice.

His career began in Accra in 2016, in IT consulting, web development and cybersecurity. Today he is the founder of Adeton AI, Director of Appiah Information Technology Systems, Country Head for Ghana at The ESG Institute, and IT & Sustainability Director at Global Volunteers Corps.

What sets the work apart is the combination: he can brief the board, build the product, and then train the team that has to use it. In a field crowded with noise, he is interested in what survives contact with reality.

02 The operating principles In his words
“

Growth cannot outpace governance.

Eric Appiah · The High Street Journal, 2025
01

Answer the governance question first.

Data handling, oversight and accountability belong in the first conversation — not the last one. AI adoption is a governance decision before it is a technology decision.

02

Move from awareness to implementation.

Training should end with a team that can do something it could not do on Monday morning. Awareness is the beginning, not the deliverable.

03

Build for here.

Ghanaian connectivity, Ghanaian devices, Ghanaian payment methods and Ghanaian regulation. Not assumptions imported from somewhere else.

04 Flagship venture 2026 · Accra

AdetonAI

A week of business development. One run.

Adeton AI is an AI sales and business-development platform for small IT and professional-services firms. Define a target market in one line. It returns real accounts — researched, qualified and scored — with the outreach already drafted.

What makes it different is what it refuses to do. Every agent output lands as a draft. Nothing reaches a prospect until a person approves it. The approval gate cannot be disabled.
